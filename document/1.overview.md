# SAGAU

## Hybrid Freelance & Local Skilled Worker Marketplace Platform

---

# 1. Project Overview

## Introduction

**Sagau** is a modern hybrid marketplace platform that connects clients with freelancers and local skilled workers.

The platform combines the strengths of global freelance platforms and local service marketplaces into a single ecosystem that supports:

- Job posting
- Worker discovery
- Application management
- Agreement-based hiring
- Reputation building
- Trust and verification systems

Sagau aims to create a reliable and transparent environment where clients can easily find qualified workers and workers can consistently discover opportunities to earn income and grow their professional reputation.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

To become the most trusted marketplace for connecting clients and skilled workers through transparency, reputation, verification, and simplified hiring workflows.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

## Challenges Faced by Clients

Many clients face difficulties when hiring workers because:

- Reliable workers are difficult to find.
- Worker qualifications are often unclear.
- Trustworthiness cannot be easily verified.
- Comparing applicants is time-consuming.
- Hiring decisions involve uncertainty and risk.

### Common Client Concerns

- Will the worker complete the job?
- Is the worker experienced?
- Can the worker be trusted?
- Is the pricing reasonable?
- What happens if disputes arise?

---

## Challenges Faced by Workers

Workers also face multiple challenges:

- Limited visibility to potential clients.
- Difficulty finding regular job opportunities.
- Lack of professional reputation systems.
- Inability to showcase skills effectively.
- Trust barriers with new clients.

### Common Worker Concerns

- Finding new clients.
- Building credibility.
- Receiving fair opportunities.
- Demonstrating experience.
- Creating a professional online presence.
