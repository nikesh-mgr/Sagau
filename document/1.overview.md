# SAGAU

## Hybrid Freelance & Local Skilled Worker Marketplace

---

# 1. Introduction

## Overview

**Sagau** is a trust-first hybrid marketplace platform that connects **clients** with **freelancers** and **local skilled workers** through a secure, transparent, and reputation-driven ecosystem.

The platform combines the strengths of global freelance marketplaces (such as Upwork and Fiverr) with local service marketplaces (such as TaskRabbit), allowing users to hire professionals for both online and location-based services.

Sagau is designed to simplify hiring while promoting accountability through agreements, reviews, verification, and reputation systems.

# 4. Problem Statement

## Challenges Faced by Clients

Hiring skilled workers often involves uncertainty and risk.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty finding reliable professionals.
- Limited information about worker qualifications.
- Lack of trust and transparency.
- Time-consuming comparison of applicants.
- Risk of fraud or poor-quality work.

### Common Questions

- Can this worker be trusted?
- Is the worker experienced?
- Will the work be completed on time?
- Is the pricing fair?
- What happens if problems occur?

---

## Challenges Faced by Workers

Workers also encounter significant obstacles when seeking opportunities.

Common challenges include:

- Limited exposure to potential clients.
- Difficulty building a professional reputation.
- Inconsistent job opportunities.
- Limited ways to showcase skills and experience.
- Trust barriers with new clients.

### Common Questions

- How can I attract more clients?
- How do I build credibility?
- How can I showcase my experience?
- How can I receive fair opportunities?
