# 4. Core Working Flow

---

# Overview

The Sagau platform follows a structured marketplace workflow that connects clients with skilled workers through job posting, application management, worker selection, agreement creation, work execution, and reputation building.

The workflow is designed to ensure:

- Transparency
- Trust
- Accountability
- Efficient Hiring
- Reputation Growth

---

# Complete Marketplace Workflow

```text id="y4zt4d"
User Registration
        ↓
Profile Creation
        ↓
Client Posts Job
        ↓
Workers Apply
        ↓
Client Reviews Applications
        ↓
Worker Selected
        ↓
Agreement Created
        ↓
Work Starts
        ↓
Work Completed
        ↓
Review Submitted
        ↓
Trust Score Updated
```

---

# Step 1 — User Registration

The workflow begins when a new user creates an account on the platform.

Users can register as:

### Client

A user who wants to hire workers for specific jobs.

### Worker

A user who wants to provide services and earn income.

---

## Registration Information

Users provide:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Password
- Role Selection

---

## Authentication Process

The system:

1. Validates user information.
2. Creates a user account.
3. Encrypts the password.
4. Generates a JWT token.
5. Logs the user into the system.

---

## Result

```text id="n6g0gv"
User Account Created
Status = Active
```

---

# Step 2 — Profile Creation

After registration, users create their profiles.

Profiles help establish credibility and improve platform matching.

---

## Worker Profile

Workers provide professional information.

### Worker Information

- Profile Photo
- Bio
- Skills
- Experience
- Certifications
- Portfolio
- Service Categories
- Location

---

## Verification Information

Workers may upload:

- Citizenship
- National ID
- Certificates
- Professional Licenses

---

## Client Profile

Clients create personal or business profiles.

### Client Information

- Name
- Business Name
- Profile Image
- Contact Information
- Address
- Description

---

## Result

```text id="w4dypc"
Profile Completion Improves Visibility
```

---

# Step 3 — Client Posts Job

Clients create job listings describing the work they need completed.

---

## Job Information

### Required Fields

- Job Title
- Description
- Budget
- Location
- Category
- Required Skills
- Deadline

---

## Example

```text id="3mrg6v"
Title: House Electrical Repair

Budget: NPR 8,000

Location: Pokhara

Deadline: 5 Days
```

---

## System Actions

The platform:

1. Validates job data.
2. Creates a new job record.
3. Publishes the job.
4. Makes the job searchable.

---

## Initial Status

```text id="zvfc65"
Job Status = OPEN
```

---

# Step 4 — Workers Apply

Workers browse available jobs and submit applications.

---

## Application Contents

Workers can submit:

- Proposal Message
- Expected Pricing
- Estimated Completion Time
- Additional Notes

---

## Application Relationships

Every application is linked to:

```text id="3a73v2"
Worker ID
+
Job ID
```

---

## System Actions

The platform:

- Stores application details.
- Updates application count.
- Notifies the client.

---

## Application Status

```text id="cl4axt"
PENDING
```

---

# Step 5 — Client Reviews Applicants

The client reviews all submitted applications.

The purpose is to identify the most suitable worker.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Clients compare:

### Worker Profile

- Skills
- Experience
- Portfolio

### Reputation

- Ratings
- Reviews
- Completed Jobs

### Trust Indicators

- Verification Status
- Response Rate
- Account History

---

## Result

Client creates a shortlist of preferred candidates.

---

# Step 6 — Worker Selection

After evaluating applicants, the client selects a worker.

---

## System Actions

When a worker is selected:

### Job Status Updated

```text id="n9v1nk"
OPEN
↓
ASSIGNED
```

### Application Status Updated

```text id="48a3l0"
Selected Worker = ACCEPTED

Other Applications = REJECTED
```

### Agreement Generation

The system automatically creates an agreement between:

- Client
- Worker

---

# Agreement Includes

- Job Details
- Timeline
- Deliverables
- Responsibilities
- Conditions

---

# Step 7 — Work Starts

The worker accepts the agreement and begins the assigned task.

---

## System Updates

### Job Status

```text id="o7bkg9"
ASSIGNED
↓
IN_PROGRESS
```

### Agreement Status

```text id="0vb4tz"
ACTIVE
```

---

## During Work

Both parties can:

- Track Progress
- View Agreement
- Receive Notifications

---

# Step 8 — Work Completion

After finishing the work, the worker submits completion confirmation.

The client reviews the completed work.

---

## Client Options

### Approve Work

If satisfied:

```text id="z7wz37"
Work Approved
```

### Request Changes

If modifications are required:

```text id="h9ph0m"
Revision Requested
```

---

## Completion Status

When approved:

### Job Status

```text id="m1v88l"
COMPLETED
```

### Agreement Status

```text id="z1jfnm"
COMPLETED
```

---

# Step 9 — Review & Rating

Once work is completed, the client provides feedback.

This is one of the most important stages because it strengthens the trust ecosystem.

---

## Review Components

### Rating

Scale:

```text id="v3z41w"
1 Star → Poor
2 Stars → Fair
3 Stars → Good
4 Stars → Very Good
5 Stars → Excellent
```

### Written Review

Examples:

- Quality of work
- Communication
- Professionalism
- Timeliness

---

# Reputation System Update

The submitted review contributes to:

- Worker Reputation
- Platform Ranking
- Trust Score
- Search Visibility

---

## Trust Score Factors

```text id="1q36o0"
Reviews
+
Ratings
+
Verification Status
+
Completed Jobs
+
Response Rate
```

---

# Final Outcome

## Benefits for Client

- Job Successfully Completed
- Verified Worker Experience
- Quality Assurance

---

## Benefits for Worker

- Reputation Growth
- Higher Ranking
- Increased Trust
- Better Future Opportunities

---

## Benefits for Platform

- Stronger Trust Network
- Improved Marketplace Quality
- Increased User Retention
- Sustainable Marketplace Growth
